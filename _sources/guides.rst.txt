User guide
==========

Follow the training workflow from inputs to saved predictions:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 70

   * - Task
     - Guide
   * - Connect your model and loss
     - :doc:`training` describes batch shapes, gradients and device selection.
   * - Prepare inputs
     - :doc:`data` covers tensors, dataframe splits and scaling.
   * - Measure progress
     - :doc:`metrics` explains metric modes, aggregation and logging flags.
   * - Extend the loop
     - :doc:`events` lists the callbacks actually emitted by the trainer.
   * - Save and reuse weights
     - :doc:`checkpoints` explains saved files and inference loading.
   * - Model conditional distributions
     - :doc:`mdn` introduces mixture density outputs and likelihood losses.
   * - Search hyperparameters
     - :doc:`hpo` introduces Optuna task configuration.

For exact signatures, use :doc:`modules`. For current limitations and their
reproduction tests, use :doc:`known-issues`.
